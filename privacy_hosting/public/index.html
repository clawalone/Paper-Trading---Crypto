Privacy Policy

PaperTrading: Crypto  |  Last updated: June 11, 2025

PaperTrading: Crypto ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you create an account:

  • Email address (used for authentication via Firebase)
  • Username / display name
  • App usage data (virtual trades, portfolio performance, XP, missions)

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To save your virtual portfolio and trading history
  • To display leaderboard rankings
  • To track mission and reward progress
  • To improve the overall app experience

No Real Financial Data

PaperTrading: Crypto is a simulator. We do NOT collect, process, or store any real financial data, bank account details, credit card information, or payment information. All balances, trades, and profits within the app are entirely virtual and hold no real-world monetary value.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) – User authentication and cloud database storage. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Binance Public API – Real-time cryptocurrency market price data. No user data is sent to Binance.

Data Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
