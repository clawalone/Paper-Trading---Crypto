Delete Your Account

We respect your right to control your data. Follow the steps below to request account deletion.

1 Send a Deletion Request

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, send an email to our support team from the email address registered with your account:

📧 acadrio.query@gmail.com

Please use the subject line: Account Deletion Request

Include the following in your email:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your username (if known)
  • Confirmation that you want all data deleted

2 What Happens Next

Once we receive your request:

  • We will verify your identity using the email address
  • Your account will be permanently deleted within 7 business days
  • You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete
⚠️ Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your virtual portfolio, trade history, XP, and missions will be lost.

3 Data Deleted vs Retained

Data Type Action Retention Period
Email address 🗑 Permanently deleted Immediately
Username / display name 🗑 Permanently deleted Immediately
Virtual portfolio & trades 🗑 Permanently deleted Immediately
XP, missions & rewards 🗑 Permanently deleted Immediately
Leaderboard ranking 🗑 Permanently deleted Immediately
Aggregate/anonymous analytics 📊 May be retained Up to 90 days (no personal info)

Alternative: Delete via the App

You can also delete your account directly from within the app:

  • Open the app → Go to Profile tab
  • Tap Settings
  • Select Delete Account
  • Confirm deletion

This will immediately and permanently delete your account and all associated data.
