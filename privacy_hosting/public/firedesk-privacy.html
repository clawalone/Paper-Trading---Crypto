Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Firedesk ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. Firedesk is an unofficial, third-party management client for Firebase.

1. Information We Access and How It Is Used

Firedesk requires access to your Google Cloud Platform account via OAuth 2.0 (specifically the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/cloud-platform scope) to function.

This access is used strictly to fetch, display, and manage your Firebase project data (such as Authentication users, Firestore documents, Cloud Functions, etc.) directly on your device.

2. No Cloud Storage or Remote Tracking

Your data belongs to you. Firedesk operates entirely on-device. We do not route your API requests through our own servers. We do not collect, harvest, store, or sell your Google Cloud tokens, Firebase project data, or personal information.

All authentication tokens provided by Google are stored securely and locally on your physical device using encrypted storage, and are used solely to communicate directly with Google's official APIs.

3. Third-Party Services

The App communicates directly with Google LLC services. By using the App, your data interactions are subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because no personal data is stored on our servers, there is no remote data for us to delete. You can revoke Firedesk's access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account Security settings (Third-party apps with account access) and removing Firedesk.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@acadrio.com.
